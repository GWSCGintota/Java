# Microservices & System Design Interview Questions

Use these as revision prompts. Try answering each question aloud before opening the answer.

## 1. Monolith vs microservices?

**Answer guide:** A monolith deploys a larger application as one unit; microservices split capabilities into independently deployable services.

## 2. What is an API Gateway?

**Answer guide:** A front door that can route requests and centralize cross-cutting concerns.

## 3. What is service discovery?

**Answer guide:** A mechanism for services to locate dynamically changing service instances.

## 4. What is a circuit breaker?

**Answer guide:** A resilience pattern that temporarily stops calls to a failing dependency.

## 5. What is retry with backoff?

**Answer guide:** Repeating transiently failed operations with increasing delays.

## 6. What is idempotency in distributed systems?

**Answer guide:** Ensuring repeated delivery of the same operation does not create unintended duplicate effects.

## 7. What is eventual consistency?

**Answer guide:** Replicas/services may temporarily disagree but converge later.

## 8. What does CAP theorem state?

**Answer guide:** Under a network partition, a distributed system must trade off between consistency and availability.

## 9. What is horizontal scaling?

**Answer guide:** Adding more machines/instances.

## 10. What is vertical scaling?

**Answer guide:** Adding more resources to one machine.

## 11. What is high availability?

**Answer guide:** Designing a system to remain operational despite component failures.

## 12. What is fault tolerance?

**Answer guide:** The ability to continue functioning correctly despite faults, often with redundancy and recovery mechanisms.
