# Behavioral Interview Questions

Use these as revision prompts. Try answering each question aloud before opening the answer.

## 1. Tell me about yourself.

**Answer guide:** Give a 60–90 second summary: current role/study, relevant Java/backend strengths, one or two accomplishments, and what role you want next.

## 2. Why do you want to work as a Java developer?

**Answer guide:** Connect your interest in backend/problem solving with concrete Java learning or project experience.

## 3. Tell me about a difficult bug you fixed.

**Answer guide:** Use STAR: situation, task, action, result. Focus on your debugging process and what you learned.

## 4. Tell me about a disagreement in a team.

**Answer guide:** Show respectful communication, evidence-based decision making, and a constructive outcome.

## 5. Describe a project you are proud of.

**Answer guide:** Explain the problem, your specific contribution, technical decisions, measurable result, and lessons learned.

## 6. Tell me about a time you missed a deadline.

**Answer guide:** Own the issue, explain communication and corrective action, and show how you prevent recurrence.

## 7. How do you learn a new technology?

**Answer guide:** Describe a repeatable method: fundamentals, docs, small exercises, project application, feedback, and review.

## 8. What are your strengths?

**Answer guide:** Choose strengths relevant to the role and support each with evidence.

## 9. What is one weakness you are improving?

**Answer guide:** Pick a genuine but manageable weakness and explain specific improvement actions.

## 10. Where do you see yourself in 3–5 years?

**Answer guide:** Show growth toward stronger engineering responsibility while staying realistic and role-aligned.
